# About Rahul Kewat

**Rahul Kewat** (also known as **rahulkewat** and **therahulkewat**) is a freelance mentor, consultant, and builder. This is his official site: [rahulkewat.io](https://rahulkewat.io/).

## What he does

- Helps freelancers, founders, and creators turn their craft into a durable business
- Shares a practical freelancing playbook based on real client work
- Builds free tools (like the [website builder](/free-tools/website-builder)) that freelancers can use without login or hosting lock-in
- Publishes travel field notes from cities he’s actually visited
